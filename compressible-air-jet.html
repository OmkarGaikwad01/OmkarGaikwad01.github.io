Fluid & Thermal

Compressible Air Jet from a Pressurized Chamber

Transient discharge of air through a short passage, with the temperature and density drop from adiabatic expansion.

Category
Fluid & Thermal
Tools
Ansys Fluent
Context
Arizona State University

Overview

Air discharging from a pressurized chamber through an outlet was simulated in 3D with a density-based solver and the ideal gas law. The study varied the chamber pressure and outlet pressure to see their effect on the jet.

Setup and method

  • Create the chamber geometry and set boundary conditions, initial conditions and solver settings.
  • Vary the initial chamber pressure and the outlet pressure.
  • Visualize velocity, temperature, density and Mach number on the plane of symmetry.
  • Interpret the transient, adiabatic expansion against compressible-flow theory.

Results

The two pressure cases give different jets: the peak Mach number along the axis is near 0.19 in one case and near 0.48 in the other.
