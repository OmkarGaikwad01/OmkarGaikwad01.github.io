Fluid & Thermal

Gas Leakage Dynamics in a Warehouse

Multiphase simulation of a methane leak and how fresh-air injection changes where the gas goes.

Category
Fluid & Thermal
Tools
Ansys Fluent · VOF multiphase
Context
Arizona State University

Overview

A natural gas leak from an underground vault into a warehouse was simulated in 2D with the volume-of-fluid (VOF) model. Two ventilation configurations were compared by tracking methane volume fraction and the D-index over time.

Setup and method

  • Domain: 9 m × 9 m warehouse with three 1 m × 1 m side pipes. The bottom pipe feeds methane from a reservoir.
  • Top pipe: pressure outlet to air at zero gauge pressure. Left pipe: closed in Case A, fresh air at 5 m/s in Case B.
  • Model: VOF with the mixture-averaged operating density method, transient run to 7 seconds.
  • Post-processing: methane volume fraction contours, mixture velocity magnitude, and D-index line plots against time.

Results

The contours show the methane plume stay symmetric in Case A and bend toward the ventilation outlet in Case B, where the fresh-air jet changes the flow pattern.
