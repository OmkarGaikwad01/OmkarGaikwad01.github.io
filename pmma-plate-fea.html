Tension-Loaded PMMA Plate: Mesh Refinement Study

Abaqus model of a notched 74 × 74 mm PMMA plate in tension, with a mesh sensitivity study.

Category
Structures & FEA
Tools
Abaqus
Context
Arizona State University

Overview

A 74 × 74 mm PMMA (plexiglass) plate, 6.35 mm thick, was modeled under a tension test. The geometry was changed from the reference part, and stress and strain fields were studied as both the mesh and the load varied.

Setup and method

  • Model the plate with suitable boundary conditions, material properties and loading.
  • Run a mesh refinement study to see how sensitive stress and strain are to element size.
  • Hold the mesh fixed and compare stress and strain fields under different loads.
  • Measure how load changes the maximum stress and strain.

Results

The deliverables are refined-mesh models and contour plots of maximum principal stress, strain and displacement for the modified plate.
