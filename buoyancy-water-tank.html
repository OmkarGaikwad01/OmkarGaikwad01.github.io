Buoyancy-Driven Convection in a Water Tank

Transient natural convection in a cylindrical tank with a heated base, run at Earth and Moon gravity.

Category
Fluid & Thermal
Tools
Ansys Fluent · SolidWorks
Context
Arizona State University
Period
Jan–May 2023

Overview

A cylindrical tank with inlet and outlet pipes was modeled in SolidWorks. Water density was allowed to vary with temperature through the Boussinesq model so buoyancy drives the flow, and the same case was run under Earth and lunar gravity.

Setup and method

  • Initial condition: tank filled with water at 50 °C. Inlet at 0.1 m/s and 20 °C; bottom plate held at 50 °C.
  • Physics: energy equation on, k-omega turbulence model, Boussinesq buoyancy with operating density 994.02 kg/m³ and thermal expansion coefficient 0.000345 1/K.
  • Two gravity cases: 9.81 m/s² (Earth) and 1.62 m/s² (Moon).
  • Time stepping: 0.5 s steps (120 iterations) for the first minute, then 1 s steps (300 iterations) out to five minutes.

Results

Outputs are velocity and temperature contours on the plane of symmetry at one and five minutes, plus the volume-averaged temperature history for each gravity case.
