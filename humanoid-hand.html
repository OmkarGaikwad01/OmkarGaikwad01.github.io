Humanoid Hand: SolidWorks Prototype and 20-DOF MATLAB Simulator

A 3D-printed humanoid hand with magnetic joints, plus an interactive MATLAB app that simulates a 20-degree-of-freedom hand.

Category
Design & Robotics
Tools
SolidWorks · 3D printing · MATLAB App Designer · SynGrasp
Context
Arizona State University
Period
Aug–Dec 2022 (prototype)

Overview

This work has two parts. The physical part is a humanoid hand designed in SolidWorks with kinematic assemblies and prototyped by 3D printing. The software part is a team-built MATLAB application that simulates a 20-DOF hand and calculates joint angles for hand signs.

Setup and method

  • Prototype: 3D CAD and kinematic assembly in SolidWorks; 3D-printed parts with magnetic joints for smooth, low-friction finger motion.
  • Wiring: electrical harness laid out in SolidWorks so wires route clear of the moving joints.
  • Simulator: interactive MATLAB app built with App Designer on top of the SynGrasp toolbox and robotics literature.
  • My part in the MATLAB team project: modifying code, fixing major errors, building the app with teammates, and analyzing hand configurations and sign angles.

Results

The app shows the hand model, joint motion paths and workspace in 3D, and the team delivered the working simulator with hand-sign angle calculations.
