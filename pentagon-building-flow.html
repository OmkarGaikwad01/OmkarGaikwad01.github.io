Aerodynamics

External Flow Around Pentagon-Shaped Buildings

Total drag on two pentagon-shaped building cases, split into pressure and viscous parts.

Category
Aerodynamics
Tools
Ansys Fluent
Context
Arizona State University

Overview

Two pentagon-shaped building cases were simulated to see how drag builds up on the geometry. The goal was to separate the pressure and viscous contributions and compare the two cases.

Setup and method

  • Run both cases and compute total drag force and its pressure and viscous components.
  • Tabulate the drag values and analyze each contribution.
  • Contours of y-velocity on a horizontal and a vertical plane for both runs.
  • For run 1, contours of turbulent kinetic energy and turbulent viscosity.

Results

Each run is reported as total drag split into pressure and viscous parts, alongside the velocity and turbulence fields that explain where the drag comes from.
