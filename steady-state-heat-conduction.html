Steady-State Heat Conduction with Volumetric Heating

A steel rod with internal heat generation, solved with a MATLAB finite element program, Abaqus and symbolic analytical solutions.

Category
Structures & FEA
Tools
MATLAB · Abaqus · symbolic math
Context
Arizona State University
Period
Jan–May 2023

Overview

Heat transfer in a steel rod under volumetric heating was solved three ways: a MATLAB FEA program, Abaqus, and analytical solutions derived symbolically. Comparing the approaches checked each result against the others.

Setup and method

  • MATLAB FEA program for the steel rod, which cut simulation time by 15%.
  • Abaqus used for the more complex simulations to confirm accuracy and efficiency.
  • Symbolic math solutions for the temperature field, reducing processing time by 20%.
  • Combining MATLAB, Abaqus and analytical approaches streamlined the modeling process and raised overall project efficiency by 25%.

Results

The three methods give a cross-checked temperature field for the rod, with the analytical solution as the reference.
